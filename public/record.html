Voice Recorder

Record the lines the game shouts when you crash. Say them like you mean it — this is the bit nobody else can do for you.

How this works
  1. Press Turn on the microphone and allow it.
  2. For each line: press Record, say it, press Stop.
  3. Press Play to hear it back. Not happy? Record it again.
  4. When they all sound good, press Download all at the bottom.
  5. Give the files to Dad — they go in public/voice/.
Microphone off

When you're done
